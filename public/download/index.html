Open Bambeh in the browser

How to install

  1. Tap “Download the app”. If Chrome warns about this type of file, confirm the download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from Chrome: open Settings, allow this source, then come back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in with your number and password.
Already have an older Bambeh app?If Android says “App not installed”, or that it conflicts with an app already on the phone, remove the old Bambeh first: press and hold its icon, App info, Uninstall. Then install again and sign in. Your account, adverts, messages and orders are kept by Bambeh, not on the phone.
It keeps itself up to dateAfter this, the app updates itself over the internet with small downloads. You will not need to install it again, except in the rare case the app asks you to.
